Fig. S2. Class-wise segmentation performance of ASPP-AG-SwinUNet with balanced sampling, CE + Tversky loss and ASPP dilation rates 1, 2 and 3. IoU, DSC and precision are computed from pooled test pixels separately for each seed. Evaluation uses the 35-image internal development evaluation subset with ground-truth-assisted 256 × 256 ROI preprocessing.
