Fig. S3. Comparison of ASPP-AG-SwinUNet with eight segmentation baselines on the internal wound dataset. Ours uses balanced sampling, CE + Tversky loss and ASPP dilation rates 1, 2 and 3. mIoU is the unweighted average of the five class IoUs, including background, computed from pooled test pixels. Circles denote six training seeds per model (1001–1006); black horizontal lines denote the mean and vertical lines denote one sample standard deviation. Models are ordered by mean mIoU. All models use the same 35-image internal development evaluation subset and ground-truth-assisted 256 × 256 ROI evaluation.
