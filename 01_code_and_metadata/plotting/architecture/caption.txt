Fig. 2B. ASPP-AG-SwinUNet architecture for the final B internal segmentation model. A 256 × 256 RGB input is encoded by four Swin Transformer V2 stages with 96, 192, 384 and 768 channels at 64 × 64, 32 × 32, 16 × 16 and 8 × 8 resolutions. ASPP combines a 1 × 1 branch, three 3 × 3 branches with dilation rates 1, 2 and 3, and an image-pooling branch, followed by concatenation and 1 × 1 projection to 768 channels. Three attention gates modulate encoder skip features using upsampled deeper features before channel concatenation. Decoder stages 3, 2, 1 and 0 produce 384, 192, 96 and 64 channels, respectively. A 1 × 1 head outputs five-class logits at 128 × 128, followed by bilinear interpolation to 256 × 256. Tensor labels denote channels × height × width; dashed orange arrows denote gate guidance. The separately trained human model retains dilation rates 6, 12 and 18 and a four-class output head, including background.
